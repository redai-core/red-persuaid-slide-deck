# Deep Client Interview Framework (Step 1)

Before drafting any slides, you must gather or extract the core strategic context across **7 core dimensions**. This framework is 100% domain-agnostic and applies equally to B2B enterprise software, B2C consumer hardware, D2C retail, healthcare, fintech, professional services, and high-growth startups.

---

## The 7 Core Dimensions

### 1. Brand & Location
- **Brand Name & Entity**: Exact brand name, holding company / backing, domain URL (`example.com`).
- **Target Geography**: Country, target metro areas, languages, and geographic constraints (e.g. "Indonesia / Jadetabek commuters", "North America Enterprise", "DACH region").
- **Brand Standing & Track Record**: Years in market, scale stats (e.g. revenue, active users, km traveled, customers served), current market tier (market leader, challenger, new entrant).

### 2. Audience & Persona
- **Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: Who signs the check or makes the final purchase decision?
- **User Personas vs Buyer Personas**: E.g., Daily commuter vs fleet manager; IT Security VP vs frontline engineer.
- **Demographics & Psychographics**: Pragmatic vs aspirational; budget-conscious vs performance-obsessed; early adopters vs risk-averse legacy buyers.

### 3. Product & USP (Unique Selling Proposition)
- **Core Product / Service Offering**: What does the product actually do?
- **The Core Moat / Unfair Advantage**: What does this brand have that competitors cannot easily copy? (e.g., proprietary battery swap network, patented algorithm, institutional trust, unique pricing model).
- **Flagship Offerings**: Main models, SKU tiers, or product editions.

### 4. Competitor Landscape
- **Primary Direct Competitors**: The top 3-5 players buyers consider.
- **Competitor Framing & Archetypes**:
  - *Player A*: Premium / design leader (high price).
  - *Player B*: Low-cost / budget player.
  - *Player C*: Legacy incumbent with massive brand trust.
  - *Player D*: Aggressive new entrant with heavy PR / venture funding.
- **Perception Gap**: Where does our brand currently sit vs where it should sit?

### 5. Customer Pain Points
- **Frustrations with Existing Alternatives**: What annoys buyers about current market options? (e.g., long charging times, opaque pricing, unreliable software, vendor lock-in).
- **Frictions in Adoption**: High upfront costs, maintenance anxiety, complex deployment.
- **Unaddressed Anxieties**: Fear of making the wrong purchase decision.

### 6. Primary Need & Jobs to be Done (JTBD)
- **Functional Need**: The pragmatic job (e.g., get from A to B cheaply; pass SOC2 compliance in 30 days).
- **Emotional & Social Need**: Peace of mind, status, looking smart to leadership, effortless reliability.
- **The Ultimate Payoff**: What does success look like for the customer after choosing this brand?

### 7. Search & Buying Behavior
- **Discovery Channels**: Where do buyers begin their search? (Google search, AI engines, Reddit/forums, industry review media, word of mouth, TikTok/YouTube).
- **Evaluation & Validation Points**: What sources do they trust for objective truth? (Editorial test reviews, peer forum threads, comparison charts, ROI calculators).
- **Decision Criteria**: Price, total cost of ownership (TCO), convenience, infrastructure availability, warranty, customer support.

---

## Interview Execution Protocol

When conducting the interview with the user:
1. **Adaptive Ingestion**: If the user provides a brief, website URL, or raw notes upfront, automatically ingest and map the known information into the 7 dimensions.
2. **Targeted Follow-up**: Ask targeted, non-redundant questions ONLY for missing or ambiguous dimensions.
3. **Structured Brief Summary**: Present the completed strategic brief in a crisp markdown table for immediate validation before transitioning to Step 2 (Narrative Architecture).
