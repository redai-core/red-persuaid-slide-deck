# Slide Archetype Catalog

This catalog defines the generalized slide archetypes extracted from high-impact executive and strategic consulting decks (such as McKinsey SCR, BCG, and Captain Words AI Visibility decks). Every slide is engineered with strict visual hierarchy: **Kicker Tag**, **Assertive Action Title**, **Visual Core (Cards/Charts/Grids)**, and an **Executive "What This Means" Synthesis Callout**.

---

## 1. Title / Cover Slide (`ARCH-TITLE`)
- **Visual Structure**: Minimalist, high-authority cover with high contrast.
- **Components**:
  - Brand Monogram / Logo mark at top.
  - Deck Category Tag (e.g. `AI VISIBILITY REPORT`, `COMMERCIAL STRATEGY`, `MARKET ENTRY PLAN`).
  - Client / Subject Name in bold primary heading.
  - Core Subtitle: The central thematic question or scope statement.
  - Scope Metrics / Metadata Bar: 3-4 key parameters (e.g., `25 category prompts | 225 data points | 3 engines analysed`).
  - Geography & Date metadata (e.g., `Market: Indonesia | Prepared by [Agency] · [Date]`).
  - Client Motto / Tagline accent.

---

## 2. Core Finding / Hero Stat Quad (`ARCH-HERO-STAT`)
- **Visual Structure**: 4-column metric grid or 2x2 hero card grid above a dual-column synthesis box.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `CORE FINDING` or `EXECUTIVE SUMMARY`
  - **Action Title**: Assertive takeaway (e.g., *"Visible but underpowered — the AI gap is real"*)
  - **Metric Grid (4 Cards)**:
    1. Hero Percentage / Primary Metric (e.g., `9.3%` - *of AI answers mention Client*)
    2. Category Ranking (e.g., `#4` - *among EV motorcycle brands*)
    3. Qualitative Score / Benchmark (e.g., `63 / 100` - *average sentiment when named*)
    4. Top-Subsegment Stat (e.g., `18%` - *of mentions place Client #1 on niche queries*)
  - **Narrative Context Block**: Paragraph detailing the underlying reality vs perception.
  - **"What this means" Callout**: High-contrast box clarifying that the gap is operational/distribution, not capability/reputation.

---

## 3. Competitor Share / Gap Bar Chart (`ARCH-GAP-BAR`)
- **Visual Structure**: Horizontal bar chart layout on the left/top with analytical breakdown cards on the right/bottom.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `THE KEY GAP` or `MARKET SHARE DISTRIBUTION`
  - **Action Title**: Comparative verdict (e.g., *"AI defaults to established players — Brand barely registers"*)
  - **Ranked Horizontal Bars / Percentages**:
    - Competitor A (e.g., `31.2%`)
    - Competitor B (e.g., `28.4%`)
    - Competitor C (e.g., `24.7%`)
    - Competitor D (e.g., `19.1%`)
    - **Subject Brand** (Highlighted with distinct accent / warning badge, e.g. `9.3%`)
    - Trailing Competitors (e.g., `7.4%`, `5.1%`)
  - **"Why does this happen?" Driver List**: 4 icon-anchored drivers explaining why competitors win (e.g., PR machine, forum presence, editorial dominance, legacy search equity).
  - **"What this means" Callout**: Strategic imperative on how to bridge the delta.

---

## 4. Breakdown by Platform / Engine / Segment (`ARCH-SEGMENT-BREAKDOWN`)
- **Visual Structure**: 3-column comparative cards comparing behavior across channels, engines, or customer segments.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `COVERAGE BY ENGINE` or `CHANNEL PERFORMANCE`
  - **Action Title**: Diagnosing root cause (e.g., *"Consistently low — a supply problem, not a platform problem"*)
  - **3 Channel Cards**:
    - Card Header: Metric badge (e.g., `10.7% | 75 responses`)
    - Channel Name: (e.g., `ChatGPT`, `Google Overviews`, `Perplexity` or `Tier 1 Enterprise`, `Mid-Market`, `SMB`)
    - Channel Specific Behavior / Citation Sources.
  - **Analytical Takeaway Pair**:
    - `⚠ Warning / Diagnostic Box`: Why single-point fixes fail.
    - `✓ Opportunity Box`: How a single cohesive workstream moves all segments simultaneously.

---

## 5. Voice of Market / Verbatim Quotation Grid (`ARCH-VOICE-GRID`)
- **Visual Structure**: 4-card or 2x2 grid of direct quotes, AI output snippets, or customer interview excerpts with giant decorative quotation marks.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `POSITIONING` or `VOICE OF CUSTOMER`
  - **Action Title**: Summary of perceptual reality (e.g., *"How AI describes Brand, in its own words"*)
  - **Quote Cards (2-4 items)**:
    - Giant quotation mark `“`
    - Exact verbatim quote text.
    - Source Attribution (e.g., `— Perplexity`, `— Enterprise IT Buyer, FinTech`).
  - **"What this means" Callout**: Synthesis explaining the perception mismatch (e.g., why the market frames them as B2B instead of B2C, or tactical instead of strategic).

---

## 6. Detailed Competitor Landscape Matrix (`ARCH-COMPETITOR-MATRIX`)
- **Visual Structure**: 5-column or vertical card split detailing competitor positioning archetypes.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `COMPETITOR LANDSCAPE`
  - **Action Title**: Structural market mapping (e.g., *"How each brand is framed — and where Client fits"*)
  - **Competitor Archetype Cards**:
    - Name + Positioning Tag (e.g., `Alva - Premium performance (31.2%)`, `Smoot - Budget swap king (28.4%)`, `Polytron - Trusted local legacy (24.7%)`)
    - Market Narrative: How they are described in the wild.
    - Channel/Distribution Strength: Where they get their authority.
    - Client Card: Highlighted with unique moat and current framing delta.

---

## 7. The Unleveraged Asset / Moat Highlight (`ARCH-MOAT-HIGHLIGHT`)
- **Visual Structure**: Left side: 4 metric callout stats on niche dominance; Right side: 3 strategic pillars of existing unfair advantages.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `THE ASSET YOU ALREADY HOLD` or `UNFAIR ADVANTAGES`
  - **Action Title**: Affirming the latent strength (e.g., *"When Brand is named, it frames it as infrastructure leader — a durable advantage"*)
  - **Niche Metric Stack (4 items)**:
    - Win rate in core sweet-spot queries (e.g., `18% place Brand #1`)
    - Top-3 presence rate (e.g., `42%`)
    - Average rank when surfaced.
    - Sentiment score comparison.
  - **Owned Assets Pillars (3 cards)**:
    - Pillar 1: Proprietary network / product capability.
    - Pillar 2: Institutional credibility / backing / partnerships.
    - Pillar 3: Real-world scale metrics / data proof.

---

## 8. Source Base / Ecosystem Matrix (`ARCH-SOURCE-MATRIX`)
- **Visual Structure**: Structured executive data table with styled column badges.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `THE SOURCE BASE` or `CHANNEL ATTRIBUTION`
  - **Action Title**: Pinpointing citation & decision drivers (e.g., *"Where these answers come from"*)
  - **Table Columns**:
    - `SOURCE TYPE` (e.g., Editorial Media, Community Forums, Video/YouTube, Owned Properties)
    - `IN THE DATA / VOLUME` (Share of citations / traffic)
    - `WHY IT MATTERS` (Role in the buyer purchase funnel)
    - `LEVER / ACTION` (Badge: `Content`, `Community`, `Monitor`, `Optimize`)
  - **"What this means" Callout**: Highlighting the primary 1-2 levers that do 80% of the heavy lifting.

---

## 9. Community & Forum Opportunity Map (`ARCH-COMMUNITY-MAP`)
- **Visual Structure**: List of real-world buyer threads or decision venues with status indicators.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `COMMUNITY THREAD MAP` or `BUYER CONVERSATION AUDIT`
  - **Action Title**: Highlighting specific missing conversations (e.g., *"The communities buyers keep citing — and where Brand is absent"*)
  - **Opportunity Cards**:
    - Channel/Sub-forum name (e.g., `r/IndonesiaEV`, `Kaskus Otomotif`)
    - Status Badge: `🔴 Brand Absent`, `🟡 Indirect Mention`, `🟢 Brand Dominant`
    - Exact Thread Title / Search Query.
    - Contextual finding: Who won the thread and what client story was missing.
  - **"What this means" Callout**: Actionable guidelines for authentic, owned community participation.

---

## 10. Strategic Action Plan / Phased Roadmap (`ARCH-ACTION-PLAN`)
- **Visual Structure**: 3 sequential numbered roadmap cards across the slide.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `THE PLAN` or `STRATEGIC ROADMAP`
  - **Action Title**: Clear sequencing (e.g., *"What we would do, in order"*)
  - **Phased Workstream Cards (Numbered 1, 2, 3)**:
    - Step Number (e.g., `1`, `2`, `3`)
    - Workstream Title (e.g., `Content Restructuring & Optimization`)
    - Priority Badge: `PRIMARY`, `COMMUNITY LEVER`, `ASSESS AFTER Q1`
    - Detailed Tactical Scope: Exact actions, formats, and channels.
    - `Maps to:` Tag directly connecting the workstream to evidence from earlier slides.

---

## 11. Operational Enablers / Always-On Infrastructure (`ARCH-ENABLERS`)
- **Visual Structure**: 4 equal columns with icon headers outlining operational rigor.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `UNDERNEATH BOTH WORKSTREAMS` or `OPERATIONAL FOUNDATION`
  - **Action Title**: Ongoing engine (e.g., *"Always-on category intelligence"*)
  - **4 Enabler Columns**:
    1. `Daily Tracking / Monitoring`: Continuous measurement vs static audits.
    2. `New-Source / Trend Discovery`: Surfacing emerging buyer hubs and queries.
    3. `Competitor-Source Visibility`: Reverse-engineering competitor wins.
    4. `Monthly Reporting & Synthesis`: Strategic readouts and backlog reprioritization.

---

## 12. Commercial Investment Scope (`ARCH-INVESTMENT`)
- **Visual Structure**: Side-by-side pricing & engagement package cards with feature checklists.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `RECOMMENDED ENGAGEMENT` or `INVESTMENT SCOPE`
  - **Action Title**: Commitment framework (e.g., *"Investment & Engagement Structure"*)
  - **Package Cards (2-3 tiers)**:
    - Workstream Name + Scope Subtitle (`On-page`, `Off-page`, `Community`)
    - Volume Specification (e.g., `6 pieces / month`, `2 owned accounts`)
    - Commitment Duration (e.g., `6-month minimum`)
    - Checklist of included deliverables with checkmarks `✓`.
    - Phased / Conditional add-ons clearly earmarked.

---

## 13. Monthly Deliverables Specification (`ARCH-DELIVERABLES`)
- **Visual Structure**: 2-3 container cards with detailed checklist items and named examples.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `DELIVERABLES`
  - **Action Title**: Concrete certainty (e.g., *"What Client receives each month"*)
  - **Workstream Breakdown Boxes**:
    - Itemized bullet points with exact counts.
    - Named concrete content/campaign examples (e.g., buyer guides, comparison calculators, subsidy explainers).
    - Governance & reporting commitments.
  - **Assurance Footer Box**: Reassuring the executive sponsor of exact counts and ownership.

---

## 14. Executive Opportunity Thesis / Concluding Vision (`ARCH-OPPORTUNITY-THESIS`)
- **Visual Structure**: Grand visual conclusion with large typography and core thesis.
- **Components**:
  - **Kicker**: `THE OPPORTUNITY` or `THE PATH FORWARD`
  - **Hero Affirmation**: Large bold statement (e.g., *"The market already knows you are real. It just doesn't tell buyers often enough."*)
  - **Synthesized Argument**: Crisp paragraph summarizing why the work is straightforward, how latent assets will be leveraged, and the decisive moat.
  - **Signature/Sign-off Monogram**.
